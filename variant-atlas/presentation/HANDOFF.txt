MARKET-GAP REVISION
Slides 14–16 add the specific workflow gap hypothesis, customer capacity value and
a bottom-up US entry-segment model. Gap demand, fit rates and willingness to pay
remain unvalidated. Market-Value-Model.xlsx is editable and includes formulas,
sensitivity cases, source links and low-use/break-even examples.
Base customer scenario: 8 cases/month × 2 net hours × $60/hour × 12 = $11,520/year
of capacity value, versus a proposed $3,600 annual lab fee. This is not measured
cash savings. Break-even: 5 net hours/month.
BIO reports more than 2,000 US biotech companies. A rounded 2,000-company pool,
assumed 10–30% eligible fit, one account per firm and $3,600 annual pricing imply
a conditional $0.72–2.16M annual subscription pool, not a forecast or global TAM.
Run source/build-market-model.py before source/build-deck.py to refresh the model.
